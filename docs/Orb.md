MALI's in-conversation presence on the kiosk — the bud, alive — and the only thing that moves there. States: `idle` (slow breathe), `listening` (teal ring expands with `level` 0-1), `thinking` (quiet dashed ring spins), `speaking` (gold halo pulses with `level`). Default 160px. Nong Mali herself greets once at the start of a session via `NongMali`; after that the Orb carries the conversation.
