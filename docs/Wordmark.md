The MALI wordmark — Anuphan bold with the L in teal. `lang="th"` renders มะลิ with ล in teal (Thai is primary). `friendly` gives the lowercase cut with the gold i-dot (Thai: gold vowel) — kiosk welcome and mascot contexts only; product surfaces use the uppercase default. `product` appends a quiet suffix ("Prescreening"). `tone="dark"` reverses the lockup for deep-teal surfaces: paper letters, gold accent letter, and the gold signature bud. Pairs with the bud at `height * 1.15`.
