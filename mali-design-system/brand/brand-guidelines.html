MALI Design System · Brand Guidelines v1.0

MALI

Multilingual health assistant for MFU Medical Center. Nong Mali greets, the bud shows progress, one teal family carries the whole product.

01 · Logos

Two marks, one system. Nong Mali is the primary mark — welcome screens, brand moments, anywhere she speaks. The bud is the secondary mark — progress, loading, favicons, small spaces.

Do
  • Keep clear space of half the mark's width on all sides
  • Use on paper #FAF9F5 or white; deep leaf #1F3B38 for dark
  • Use the bud whenever the mark is smaller than 40px
Don't
  • Recolour outside the approved palettes, stretch, rotate or outline
  • Place on photos or busy patterns
  • Show triage colours anywhere near the marks on kiosk screens

02 · Wordmark

Anuphan Bold. Teal L is the accent. Uppercase for product surfaces; the lowercase gold-dot version for friendly moments (kiosk welcome, mascot contexts). In Thai, มะลิ mirrors the system: ล takes the teal like the L, and the vowel ิ can take the gold like the i-dot.

07 · Background texture

Waiting and loading screens never sit on blank paper. The texture is drawn from the mark — petals and stamen dots at whisper opacity. It must disappear behind content: if you notice it, it is too strong.

Recommended
A · Petal drift
Scattered jasmine petals + gold dots · welcome & waiting screens
B · Stamen dots
Quiet dot grid · portal empty states, behind tables
C · Leaf weave
Wide diagonal bands · print covers, large banners only

Preparing your next question
Rules. Texture appears only when content is sparse — welcome, waiting, loading, empty states. Never behind a question, a form or a table body. Petals stay teal at ≤14% opacity, dots gold at ≤30%; the pattern never animates — motion belongs to the bud alone, drawing itself over it.
