Nong Mali — the primary mark. She appears on welcome screens and brand moments, anywhere MALI speaks as a character: greet once per session (`motion="nongBloom"` for the approved bloom entrance), never as looping decoration. Use 80px or larger; below 40px switch to the bud (`Mark`). Fixed palette — never recolour; place only on paper `#FAF9F5` or white.

Motion: pass `motion="nongBloom" | "nongRise" | "nongWave" | "nongRiseSway"`, or an attract loop — `"nongShowreel"` | `"nongWaveHello"` | `"nongBounce"` | `"nongExplode"` | `"nongHeartbeat"` — which throw rings and petals into the mark's parent and run forever. See [[Motion]].
